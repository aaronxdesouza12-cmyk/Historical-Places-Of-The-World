Who I Am

Hi! I'm Aaron De Souza, a passionate web developer and history enthusiast. This website was built to celebrate the timeless beauty and cultural heritage of the world’s most iconic historical monuments.

From the architectural wonder of the Taj Mahal to the grandeur of the Colosseum and the majesty of the Great Wall of China, I wanted to create a space where history feels alive and accessible. Each page of this project represents my interest in art, design, and the stories that connect humanity across time.

My Mission

To inspire curiosity and appreciation for the world’s historic landmarks through technology and storytelling. My goal is to create engaging digital experiences that bring ancient civilizations closer to modern audiences.
